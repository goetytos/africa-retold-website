Privacy Notice

How we handle information you share with us.

Home / Privacy

Last updated 22 July 2026

Who We Are

Ubuntu Retold Initiatives CBO is a community-based organization in Kasarani, Nairobi, Kenya. You can contact us at ubunturetold@gmail.com or +254 797 733 722.

Information We Collect

We collect information you choose to provide through our contact, volunteer, and newsletter forms. This may include your name, email address, phone number, area of interest, message, and volunteer motivation.

How We Use It

We use your information to respond to enquiries, review volunteer applications, send updates you requested, coordinate programmes, and maintain necessary organizational records. We do not sell personal information.

Service Providers

Website forms are delivered through FormSubmit. Our website is hosted by Vercel. These providers may process technical and submitted information to deliver their services under their own privacy terms.

Retention and Security

We retain information only as long as reasonably needed for the purpose for which it was collected, legal obligations, and organizational records. We take reasonable measures to limit access, but no internet transmission is completely secure.

Your Choices

You may ask us to correct or delete information you submitted, or unsubscribe from updates, by emailing ubunturetold@gmail.com. We may retain information where required by law or legitimate record-keeping needs.

Children's Privacy

This website is intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly request personal information directly from children through the website. Event photos involving children are published for programme storytelling only where the organization has a lawful basis and appropriate authorization.

Changes

We may update this notice as our services change. The latest revision date will appear at the top of this page.
