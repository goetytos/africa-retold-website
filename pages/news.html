Africa Day at Mama Fauzia Children's Home — Recap Past Event

Past Event · Sunday, 24 May 2026 · Mama Fauzia Children's Home, Nairobi

Theme: “Celebrating African Unity through Love, Service & Community.”

This Africa Day, Ubuntu Retold Initiatives chose to celebrate the continent the way Ubuntu teaches us to — together, with the community. We spent the day at Mama Fauzia Children's Home in Nairobi, where love, learning, and laughter filled every moment.

We sat with the children and mentored them, opened conversations about Pan-Africanism — who we are as a continent, the leaders who shaped us, and the future they themselves are building — and taught them about environmental conservation, why our trees, our soil, and our rivers matter. We presented gifts of food, toiletries, and supplies, did face painting, and shared tree seedlings as a small symbol of the future we are planting together.

It was a day of joy, of presence, and of reminding the children of Mama Fauzia that they are seen, loved, and part of a much bigger African story. I am because we are.

💙 Thank you

To everyone who donated food, clothes, toiletries, school supplies and tree seedlings — and to every volunteer who showed up: asanteni sana. You made this day possible. To the staff and children of Mama Fauzia Children's Home — thank you for welcoming us into your home and your hearts.

Standing Against the Tide — Ubuntu Retold at the Flood Resistance Event

Community Impact · April 16, 2026 · By Ubuntu Retold Initiatives

On Thursday, 16 April 2026, our Chairperson represented Ubuntu Retold Initiatives at a flood resistance event — a timely and critical conversation around climate resilience and community preparedness. The event brought together stakeholders committed to finding lasting solutions to the flooding challenges that affect many Nairobi residents.

Our Chairperson's presence reaffirmed our commitment to environmental justice and community advocacy. Ubuntu Retold remains dedicated to being at the table wherever our communities are being discussed and defended.
