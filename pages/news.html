Africa Day Children's Home Visit — Mama Fauzia Upcoming

Upcoming Event · Sunday, 24 May 2026 · From 10:00 AM

Theme: “Celebrating African Unity through Love, Service & Community.”

This Africa Day, Ubuntu Retold Initiatives is heading to Mama Fauzia Children's Home for a day dedicated to love, service, and community. We invite you to join us in creating a memorable, meaningful experience for the children — together we'll spread love, create smiles, and build hope.

Activities for the day: face painting · poetry & storytelling · music & dance · cooking & sharing meals · tree planting · historic games · mentorship & Pan-African history · presentation of gifts

💙 Donations needed

Dry foodstuffs (rice, maize flour, beans, cooking oil, sugar) · clothes & shoes (children's sizes) · toiletries & sanitary towels · school supplies (books, pens, pencils, bags) · toys & games · fruits & snacks · tree seedlings · monetary support for transport, meals & activities.

Standing Against the Tide — Ubuntu Retold at the Flood Resistance Event

Community Impact · April 16, 2026 · By Ubuntu Retold Initiatives

On Wednesday April 16th, 2026, our Chairperson represented Ubuntu Retold Initiatives at a flood resistance event — a timely and critical conversation around climate resilience and community preparedness. The event brought together stakeholders committed to finding lasting solutions to the flooding challenges that affect many Nairobi residents.

Our Chairperson's presence reaffirmed our commitment to environmental justice and community advocacy. Ubuntu Retold remains dedicated to being at the table wherever our communities are being discussed and defended.
