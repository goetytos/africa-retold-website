Fifty Trees Initiative Upcoming

Join Ubuntu Retold Initiatives CBO and Kasarani Primary School as we plant and commit to nurturing 50 trees in recognition of World Nature Conservation Day. You can support the initiative by participating, contributing seedlings or tree-care materials, or helping fund planting and follow-up care.

📍 Kasarani Primary School · 📅 Friday, 24 July 2026 · Join or support the initiative

24

Africa Day at Mama Fauzia Children's Home Recap

Theme — “Celebrating African Unity through Love, Service & Community.” This Africa Day we celebrated at Mama Fauzia Children's Home — mentoring the children, presenting gifts, doing face painting, and teaching them about Pan-Africanism and environmental conservation. A day of love, learning, and laughter.

📍 Mama Fauzia Children's Home · 📅 Sunday, 24 May 2026 · Read the full recap & see photos

Langata Sub County Mega Cleanup Completed

Theme — “A Cleaner Today, A Greener and Better Tomorrow.” This community mega cleanup was organised by The Nairobi West Hospital and completed on 25 April 2026. Ubuntu Retold Initiatives participated as invited partners alongside Nairobi City County, Strathmore University, and other community partners.
